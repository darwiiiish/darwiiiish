## 👤 Professional Overview

I am an AI Engineer specializing in machine learning, deep learning, NLP, and intelligent robotic systems. Currently pursuing my degree in Artificial Intelligence at the Arab Academy for Science and Technology (AAST), I bring a strong track record of developing end-to-end AI applications—from fine-tuning custom models and orchestrating multi-agent LLM pipelines to building computer vision systems for real-time telemetry.

My work heavily revolves around leveraging data-driven approaches to solve complex problems, combining strong algorithmic foundations with modern MLOps and cloud deployments.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Developing scalable multi-agent workflows (LangGraph, Gemini, Groq) and hybrid AI/IoT monitoring systems.
- 🌱 **Research Interests:** Deep Reinforcement Learning for robotics, Swarm Intelligence, and Computer Vision.
- ⚡ **Recent Work:** Architected WhatsApp-based real-time predictive agents and fine-tuned summarization models for enterprise data analytics.
- 🏆 **Achievements:** 5th Place at RoboCup Rescue Maze (2024) and Technical Judge for RoboCup (2026).
